**Beercamp is back.** After a long hibernation, the informal meetup for Thailand's tech community returns to OpenCraft on Thursday, 12 November 2026, from 19:00 to 23:45.

The idea is simple: good beer, relaxed conversation about tech and business, and a room where the people who have spent the last two decades building the Thai tech scene can catch up — and where newer members of the community can meet them.

## From Barcamp to Beercamp

The story starts with Barcamp, the unconference that helped spark a generation of Thai tech people. Those early gatherings grew into a community of founders, engineers, operators, and policy specialists who went on to build businesses and shape the local tech ecosystem.

Beercamp came next: a monthly — or so — meetup for geeks to talk tech, drink beer, and build community in a more informal setting. After a long pause, it is returning for an evening of drinks and conversation.

## Who it's for

Beercamp is for the OG tech leaders, business owners, and policy people who have been part of the scene for years — and for anyone newer who wants to meet them. The point is to reconnect, trade stories, and keep knowledge moving across generations.

There is no formal agenda: just beer and good conversation in a relaxed room.

## Come for the beer, stay for the stories

Whether you have been in Thai tech since the Barcamp days or you are just getting started, you are welcome. Registration is required, so sign up in advance.
